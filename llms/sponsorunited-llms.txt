# SponsorUnited

> SponsorUnited is the leading global sports and entertainment sponsorship intelligence platform. Founded in 2018 and headquartered in Stamford, CT, it is a B2B SaaS product that empowers brands, rights holders (teams, leagues, venues, events), agencies, and media companies to discover, evaluate, negotiate, and activate marketing partnerships using data and AI.

The platform is described as an "AI-native operating system for sponsorship" and is often referenced as "the Bloomberg Terminal for marketing partnerships." SponsorUnited does not offer a free plan, public pricing, or a free trial. All access is through a paid subscription with custom pricing.

Key data scale (as of 2026):
- 403,000+ brands tracked
- 2.2M+ sponsorship deals tracked
- 21.1M+ data points across sports, entertainment, media, and talent
- $30B+ in verified deal data (used for SPND pricing models)
- Operates in 25+ countries

Credentials: SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, Inc. 5000 honoree three consecutive years (2022–2024), Series A funding ($35M) from Spectrum Equity in November 2022.

## Core Platform

- [Platform Overview](https://www.sponsorunited.com/platform): Full description of the SponsorUnited platform (v4.0), including AI features, data infrastructure, and the Living Intelligence Network
- [SPND — Sponsorship Price of Negotiated Deals](https://www.sponsorunited.com/products/spnd): First-of-its-kind pricing transparency product built on $30B+ in verified deal data; covers NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, MLS, College Sports, F1, WNBA, NWSL
- [Surface (AI Analyst)](https://www.sponsorunited.com/platform): Integrated AI assistant trained on SponsorUnited's full data set; answers sponsorship questions and surfaces insights without manual digging
- [Proposal Evaluator](https://www.sponsorunited.com/platform): AI tool that analyzes uploaded sponsorship proposals for audience fit, competitive landscape, asset/pricing benchmarks, and negotiation guidance
